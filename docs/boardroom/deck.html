Muse

An executable music format — the score is fixed, the interpretation is the work, and a performance is an event, not a render.

Board briefing — 2026-08. Sources: FORMAT_SPEC.md, docs/vision.md, docs/pipeline.md.

Thesis

Interpretation is the product, not generation.

  • A .mu file carries three things: the score (the fixed work), the prompt (the sanctioned interpretive space), and a plaintext rights manifest.
  • The deterministic player is the free baseline — the score plays identically everywhere, forever.
  • The LLM player is the musician — it reads score + prompt and brings the work to life, slowly, deliberately.
  • A performance is an event, not a render.

The event framing

Precedent is the pitch.

  • Illiac Suite, 1957 — a computer composed string quartets; the argument filled the room.
  • Gould/Bernstein, 1962 — the conductor disowned the interpretation on stage; the controversy was the coverage.
  • Our evening: the deterministic player, then the LLM conductor — same score, same sanctions, a reading. The hall is where the argument happens.

The five-second test

Play five seconds of a WAV and the room knows which is which.

  • The deterministic baseline is metronomic — correct, and instantly recognizable as mechanical.
  • The LLM-conducted render breathes — phrasing, rubato, dynamic intent from the seed.
  • That gap, audible in five seconds, is the entire product claim.

Architecture

score (fixed) ┐ seed (prompt) ├─ .mu container ─→ LLM conductor ─→ mockup ─→ render ─→ audio manifest ─────┘ ▲ deterministic baseline (P2) — the root system, free, identical everywhere
  • The score is math; the prompt is where the intelligence lives.
  • The mockup is confidential pipeline state (D20) — the public surfaces are the .mu and the rendered audio.

Business model

Open the format, keep the instruments.

  • Open: the spec, the encoding, the deterministic player — the PDF move.
  • Proprietary: the seed workbench and the LLM player — the Acrobat.
  • Licensed content: sanctioned prompt philosophies per work; provenance is mandatory and plaintext.

Competitive landscape

The conductor role is unclaimed.

  • Generators (Suno/Udio-class) produce finished audio — no score, no interpretation layer, no rights manifest.
  • Notation/DAW tools render scores mechanically — the baseline, not the musician.
  • Nobody ships the interpretive space as a first-class, licensed, verifiable artifact. That is the moat.

Roadmap

The corpus is the ratchet: Bach → Byrd → Schubert → Beethoven 5 → Beethoven 9.

  • Each rung gates the next; the Ninth is the v1.0 target (239k notes, vocal text, the full ensemble).
  • The event — one corpus work, performed by the LLM player, judged by ear as worth a hall — is the terminus.
  • Every phase is evidence-gated: a construct without corpus evidence doesn't ship.
