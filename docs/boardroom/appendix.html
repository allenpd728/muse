Conformance — "identical everywhere, forever" is enforced, not claimed

Thirteen committed .mu golden vectors (the whole corpus) are decoded through the reference decoder on every gate run; each is pinned by the sha256 of its canonical event stream. A decoder that drifts fails CI byte-for-byte. The store is 272K for 13 works. tools/muse_ci — P3, #212

The mockup never leaves the pipeline

The mockup — the LLM's full interpretation, dense performance data — is confidential pipeline state. A leaked mockup is a leaked performance: anyone holding it plus a sample renderer holds the reading. The public surfaces are the .mu (score + seed + plaintext manifest) and the rendered audio. Nothing else crosses the boundary by default; the one export path in the docs (DAWproject) is founder-gated. decision D20

No model training in the product path

The conductor is a stock, swappable LLM steered by prompt alone — seed + score + instructions. No fine-tuning, no training runs, no weights to defend. Consequences: every new model generation upgrades the conductor for free (L3 exists to A/B the same work across models), and the intelligence lives in the format, not the model — the seed carries the interpretation. Fine-tuning is a documented plan-B escape hatch, founder-decided, with evidence. decision D7

The ear gates quality; metrics support it

Every render is evaluated by ear against the known scores. Automated probes catch what the ear might miss on a first pass — budget drift, coverage shrink, assertion regressions, byte instability — but musicality is a listening verdict, not a CI gate. There is no "less musical" test, deliberately. decision D11

Provenance is mandatory and plaintext

Every .mu manifest records source, license, and AI involvement in plaintext — readable without tooling. No artist lookalikes without an explicit license record. In the post-settlement landscape, the human-authored seed is a registrable work: the architecture is a legal asset, not a liability. FORMAT_SPEC §7, D12/D13
